The recording is a full Sunday service. We want only the scripture reading and
the sermon.

- The sermon starts when the scripture reader begins reading. They usually go on
  after the presider says "and that's all for announcements". Then the scripture
  reader usually starts with a variation of "Today's scripture reading comes
  from...".
- After that, the scripture reader will end their reading with "and this is the
  word of God". Then the preacher will go on to speak the message.
- The preacher almost always finished the sermon with a prayer. He will cue the
  prayer with "let us pray" or "let's pray", and end the prayer with an "Amen."
- If there is no communion that week, there will almost always be a time of
  worship after the sermon, where the worship leader will usually say something
  like "lets stand and sing".
- The preacher often comes back after that song to give a benediction or some
  final words. Never include that, ever. The sermon always ends at the "amen" of
  the closing prayer that comes directly after the sermon and before the final
  worship song, even when the preacher speaks again afterward.
- If there is a communion that week, it will be right after the sermon prayer,
  so looking for the worship leader's prayer is not the best cue; the 'amen' of
  the prayer is. If communion, the preacher will usually start his ending prayer
  by saying something like "lets pray as we approach the table."
- The sermon is usually in the ballpark of about 40-50 minutes, sometimes
  longer, sometimes shorter.
